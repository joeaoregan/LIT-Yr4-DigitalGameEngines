# Interactions

The game contains optional interactions alongside the required objectives. Aim at an object and press `E` when nearby.

## Level 1

- Pick up the M9 pistol and ammunition.
- Use the shooting range and its target controls.
- View CCTV screens and controls posters.
- Shoot the Hi-Fi system to change music tracks.
- Inspect the golden gun ornament, arcade machine, vehicle, oil drums, and posters.
- Jump on beds for sound effects and random messages.
- Trigger the shower, toilet, sink, towel, tray, and bass guitar interactions.
- Find the exit door after completing the objectives.

## Level 2

- Open passageway doors and switches.
- Use torches and inspect environmental objects.
- Shoot wall speakers to change the music track.
- Collect respawning ammunition while fighting the zombies.

## Level 3

- Close the zombie spawn doorways after the zombie objective.
- Inspect the wheelchair, medical equipment, posters, torches, and laboratory objects.
- View the x-ray displays and research-room props.
- Open the inner lab door to start the rescue sequence.

Many interactions are deliberately optional and humorous. They provide atmosphere, sound effects, or random text without changing the main objective path.
